Deep Dive into Traffic Patterns

Week 5 Session

Traffic analysis focuses on communication patterns—who, when, and how much—rather than just the content.

What is Traffic Analysis?

Traffic analysis examines the patterns and characteristics of network communications rather than the actual content. It answers questions like:

  • Who is communicating with whom?
  • When are communications happening?
  • How much data is being transferred?
  • What are the communication patterns?

Key Patterns to Identify

Scanning Patterns

Network scanning often shows distinctive patterns:

  • Sequential port scans (checking ports in order)
  • SYN packets without corresponding ACK responses
  • Multiple connection attempts to different hosts
  • Unusual traffic volumes from single sources

Exfiltration Patterns

Data exfiltration can be identified through:

  • Unusually large outbound data transfers
  • Persistent connections to external IPs
  • Traffic during off-hours
  • Protocols not typically used for legitimate purposes

Analysis Techniques

Effective traffic analysis involves:

  • Flow Analysis: Examining NetFlow or similar flow records to identify communication patterns
  • Statistical Analysis: Looking for anomalies in traffic volume, timing, or destinations
  • Protocol Analysis: Identifying unusual protocol usage or protocol tunneling
  • Time-based Analysis: Correlating events with timestamps to build a timeline

Practical Application

In practice, traffic analysis on PCAP files can reveal:

  • Initial compromise vectors
  • Lateral movement within networks
  • Data exfiltration attempts
  • Command and control communications
